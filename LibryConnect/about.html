About LibryConnect

Connecting readers with their next great adventure since 2020

Our Mission

LibryConnect was founded with a simple vision: to make library resources accessible to everyone, anywhere. We believe that knowledge should not be confined to physical walls, and that every reader deserves seamless access to books, research materials, and literary resources.

Our platform bridges the gap between readers and libraries, enabling seamless book searches, holds, and loans across multiple library systems.

What We Do

  • Unified Search: Search across multiple library catalogs from a single, intuitive interface
  • Hold Management: Place holds on books and manage your queue with ease
  • Loan Tracking: Keep track of your borrowed items and due dates
  • Member Profiles: Personalize your experience with custom reading lists and history

Our Values

Accessibility

We believe library resources should be available to all, regardless of location or physical ability.

Simplicity

Finding and borrowing books should be straightforward, not complicated.

Community

Libraries are community hubs—we help strengthen those connections digitally.

Innovation

We continuously improve our platform to serve readers better.

The LibryConnect Team

We're a passionate team of librarians, developers, and book lovers working together to transform how people access library resources. Our diverse backgrounds allow us to understand both the technical and human aspects of library services.
