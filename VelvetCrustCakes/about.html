Our Story

From small-batch bakes to celebration centerpieces, we craft cakes that feel as special as your moment.

Velvet Crust Cakes

Velvet Crust Cakes began as a simple idea: make cakes that taste as beautiful as they look. We believe every celebration deserves a centerpiece made with care, quality ingredients, and a little imagination.

We bake in small batches and focus on balanced flavors: rich but not heavy, sweet but not overwhelming. From classic favorites to custom designs, we work closely with you to match your theme, serving size, and flavor preferences.

Whether you're planning a birthday, wedding, anniversary, or just craving something special, we're here to make it unforgettable—one slice at a time.
